# Design System: Asociación Aconiño

## Overview

**Creative North Star: “Cercana y esperanzadora.”**

The existing public site pairs direct, readable language with a confident navy-and-yellow identity, real program photography, and clear calls to action. The pages use Manrope for both display and body text. Preserve that recognizable identity and keep each page approachable, encouraging, and easy to navigate.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Clear and warm, with navy carrying trust and yellow marking important actions.
- Large, weighty headings paired with comfortable body copy.
- Real photography of Aconiño's current work and community.

## Colors

The current palette is built around deep navy, clear blue, and warm yellow, supported by white and blue-tinted neutrals.

### Primary
- **Aconiño Navy**: Primary headings, navigation, and strong background fields.
- **Aconiño Blue**: Secondary headings and supporting emphasis.
- **Aconiño Yellow**: Primary actions and small visual emphasis.

### Neutral
- **White**: Main reading surface.
- **Ink**: Default dark body text.
- **Blue Gray 50 and 100**: Quiet section backgrounds.
- **Blue Gray 200**: Dividers and control borders.
- **Blue Gray 500**: Secondary body text and captions.

## Typography

Manrope is used throughout. Display and section headings are bold or extra-bold, with tight tracking and compact leading. Body copy stays medium weight with a relaxed line-height for longer Spanish text.

## Layout

Pages center content within a wide container and use generous vertical spacing. Desktop layouts pair text and imagery in split compositions; narrow screens stack those regions in reading order. Keep navigation and key actions visible without shrinking body text.

## Elevation & Depth

Use soft, blue-tinted shadows sparingly to distinguish important controls and floating interface elements. Large content regions rely on contrast and spacing more than elevation.

## Shapes

Buttons use pill-shaped corners. Image and content surfaces use rounded corners, typically between 12 and 32 pixels. Dividers are thin and understated.

## Components

- **Primary action:** Yellow pill with navy bold text and a soft navy shadow.
- **Secondary action:** White or transparent pill with a restrained navy border.
- **Photography:** Use genuine Aconiño images, and identify the current site or program accurately when needed.
- **Navigation:** The shared site header, including the prominent Apóyanos action, anchors public pages to the existing website.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** make the main action easy to find and understand.
- **Do** use optimistic, respectful language about autonomy and participation.
- **Do** distinguish project plans and targets from completed work and results.
- **Don't** present the planned Centro Día as an operating facility.
- **Don't** imply that a current Aconiño photograph depicts the future Centro Día.
