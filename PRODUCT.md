## Users

The supplied Centro Día copy identifies families and caregivers of young people and adults with disabilities, individual donors, and companies seeking a social-impact project. Treat the priority among these audiences as an inference from the campaign copy, not a confirmed organization-wide hierarchy.

## Product Purpose

The Aconiño public website explains the association's work, programs, and ways to get in touch or contribute. The Centro Día campaign describes a planned space in Bogotá for young people and adults with disabilities, with rehabilitation, adapted technology, creative activity, and participation in daily life.

## Positioning

The campaign connects Aconiño's stated 36 years of experience with a proposed Centro Día that brings movement, communication, autonomy, creativity, and participation together in one place. The facility is being planned; do not imply that it is already operating.

## Operating Context

The website is Spanish-language and built with Astro. The existing Apóyanos route is linked from the site navigation and is the current entry point for Centro Día support. Campaign visitors may want to understand the project, contribute personally, or explore a corporate partnership.

## Brand Commitments

The name Aconiño and the existing association logo are present on the public site. The campaign copy uses Spanish and speaks about disability through autonomy, participation, communication, and everyday life. The site currently uses a navy, blue, and yellow identity.

## Product Principles

- Preserve opportunities for young people and adults with disabilities as their needs change over time.
- Connect therapy and technology to meaningful participation in everyday life.
- Respect each person's autonomy and ways of communicating.
- Describe project targets as goals and publish fundraising progress only when verified figures are available.
